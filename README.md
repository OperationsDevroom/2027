# Call for Proposals: Operations Devroom @ FOSDEM 2027

We are excited to announce the Operations Devroom, a new track at FOSDEM 2027 for SREs, DevOps & SecOps Engineers, Incident Responders, and anyone who keeps the lights on!

This devroom complements established tracks that excel at showcasing how open-source technology works by creating a dedicated space to discuss how it actually survives in production. We aim to build a "stories from the machine room" anthology, capturing the messy, real-world reality of operating these systems day-to-day.

Whether you are a solo administrator creatively managing a small deployment or part of a team pushing software to its limits in complex environments, we want to hear how you keep your systems standing!

## Content Guidelines

We invite talks that share real-world experiences operating systems at any scale, along with hard-earned lessons and practical recommendations. Our goal is to showcase the truth of running open-source infrastructure in production.

Key topics:
- **Real-World Stories:** Post-mortems of outages and security incidents - how you recovered, adapted, and what you learned.
- **Scaling Challenges:** Practical lessons from growing a system from a single server to hundreds or thousands.
- **Small Scale, Big Impact:** Creative solutions for managing critical infrastructure with limited budgets or small teams.
- **Maintainability & Security:** Techniques for building systems that are easy to operate, debug, harden, and upgrade over time.
- **Tooling & Workflows:** How you use (or abuse) open-source tools to solve operational headaches, focusing on the workflow rather than just the tool itself.

## Practical Info

- **Language:** All talks must be delivered in English.
- **Duration:** The standard format is a 20-minute talk followed by a 5-minute Q&A (25 minutes total), though we may allocate longer slots for topics of special interest.
